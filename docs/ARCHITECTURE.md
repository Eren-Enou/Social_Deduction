# Architecture

Implemented: `src/lib.rs` contains domain types, baseline policies, validation, deterministic PRNG, phase resolver, and append-only reports. `src/main.rs` provides single-run and evaluation CLI modes.

Engine truth lives in Report/objective events and is never passed to Policy. AgentView owns only ID, legal roster, phase, remaining opportunities, local observation/belief, and public claims. Listener claims contain no belief, intent, observation provenance, or objective truth. Decision audit snapshots include policy input, action, communication intent, and acceptance; resolver events record effects. Policies receive no audit reference. A caller implementing a custom policy must not inject external truth; the interface cannot prevent malicious host-side side channels.

Ballots are collected without publishing them until all decisions finish. No mutable report is returned during execution. Serialization contains no clocks, hash iteration, or platform paths. Full reports intentionally expose observer secrets and must not be fed back to agents.

IndividualState separates original observation, participation briefing, candidate belief, and incentives. Its AgentView contains one own state and public Message values only. Choice records intent, option scores, and mechanism at decision time. Baselines retain only a private `spoken` flag (logged through the budget-used mechanism and preceding decisions); all other inputs are explicit in the view. The engine independently classifies the delivered action against the recorded local belief and world actor. Unsubstantiated intent labels are retained as such. Public uncertainty is a message without an asserted candidate. Custom policies are labeled unversioned and require caller-managed provenance for reproducibility. Separate finite local state and append-only observer records preserve historical observations even when the initial briefing supplies a different belief. See experiments/002/specification.md for formulas and fixed assumptions.

The implementation is headless and uses no new dependencies.

Observer Lineage separately retains validated private acquisition or exact received-copy basis, actual parent message IDs, sorted acquisition/construction roots and bounded depth. Inference roots use recorded candidate-support assessments. Decision-time choices and listener snapshots precede effects; delivery events enumerate actual recipients. Selected origins reference decisions; injected origins reference controller interventions. Acquisitions measure separate assignments, not independent real-world witnesses. Report storage never becomes an agent memory lookup.

Config declares scoped routes, order, original observations, separate listener/communication/judgment selections and injections. Validation bounds discussion opportunities, controller messages, IDs, routes, source references, exact copies and causal depth. Each individual retains only its delivered messages for the finite game; no eviction or long-term storage mechanism is introduced. Policies and listeners are replaceable, while custom callers remain responsible for external side channels and implementation provenance. Serialized size metrics quantify message copies and audit values without claiming RAM or timing measurements.

Experiment 005 lives in `src/experiment005/`, with model, listener, engine and evaluation modules plus its separate CLI. AgentView adds legitimate own sent records, current belief, opportunities, permitted recipients and local correction costs. Policies never receive Report, Config, another individual's observations, intent labels, actual roots or correctness flags.

Later observations are scoped controller deliveries before declared decision slots; each gets an immutable acquisition record. The latest own observation supersedes the weight-2 private anchor without editing older observations; an explicit participation briefing retains weight-6 precedence. Both listener variants share this new private-input mechanism. Frozen004 projects only original assertions/uncertainty into the unchanged 004 listener and records typed revisions as ignored. The revision listener builds visible own-speaker branches through explicit earlier target links, computes current leaves, and records standing separately from immutable Message. Competing replacements dispute a branch; a retraction supplies no replacement. Valid revisions retain their initial perceived group. Conflicting active members dispute a group rather than increase corroboration. A speaker cannot revise another speaker's source statement or stale relay. Missing original/forwarded messages remain missing; nothing reads the audit to repair access. Claimed associations and copied message relationships never verify truth or actual origin.

Sincere/Selective/Misleading policies may use a second communication opportunity for one revision; Retain keeps frozen once-spoken behavior. RelayRevision can forward an exact legitimately received revision by revising its own earlier relay, subject to current candidate agreement and local cost. Choice snapshots record recognized mismatch, available forwarding reference, scores and intent independently from actual selected disclosure. Separate replaceable judgments execute ballots.

Observer Lineage distinguishes evidence parents/roots from revision_of historical dependency; replacement evidence does not inherit the old assertion as support merely because it targets it. Evidence depth is capped at three. Testimony attaches intent, source belief, classification and correctness outside wire content. Controller injections carry distinct origins and preserved resolver input snapshots, while selected messages reference actual decisions. All assignments, observations, updates, decisions, deliveries, resolutions and outcomes are appended. Config caps injections and later observations at 12 each, retains 12 individual communication opportunities and six ballots, and versions two scoped route tables. Finite received and sent histories have no evictions. No dependencies, ECS, reputation or long-term memory were introduced.

Experiment 006 lives in `src/experiment006/`: series.rs wraps the unchanged 005 engine with fresh incident state/policies, stable six identities and bounded cross-incident local memory; memory.rs defines received experiences and a replaceable CredibilityUpdater; cognition.rs adapts the unchanged revision listener; evaluation.rs defines the preregistered fixture families. Its CLI can generate an example config, execute a series or evaluate all families. No dependencies or ECS were added. Incident messages, observations, decisions and events retain local indices inside an episode envelope; persistent references pair episode and local message ID.

Only each observer's own six credit summaries enter its listener adapter. Current incident ListenerView remains the unchanged narrow 005 interface. Communication/judgment policies see current belief, so credit can indirectly affect chosen testimony, but they never receive prior Report, objective history, another observer's memory or a verification schedule. Each callback stores its actual credit input and weighted/unweighted result beside the corresponding engine Update. Frozen005 executes the identical local-memory subsystem but does not use credibility for evidence weights. Custom updaters receive one observer's FeedbackView only and are labeled caller-supplied-unversioned; validation checks bounded output and identity without claiming a sandbox for malicious host code.

After each finished incident, ingest only that observer's actually received messages and unchanged 005 visible interpretation. FIFO capacity is eight perceived-source experiences; each preserves local received claims, standing, source eligibility, original/final candidate and separate relay reports. Original-source responsibility requires the source's own assertion to have been received. Claimed named sources and hidden roots cannot supply it. Detail evictions are explicit; separately retained bounded score/counter consequences survive. Late verification never reconstructs evicted cognition from audit. All ingestion/feedback inputs, outputs and evictions remain in the observer report for causal tracing.

Scoped feedback is a declared controller intervention after a completed incident. A lab-verification-006-v1 token legally reveals its exact actor to selected recipients. Integrity assumes a trusted controller; no cryptographic protocol or natural discovery is claimed. Participant claims and collective verdicts have separate unauthenticated variants and cannot change factual credit. Evidence updates a retained source's final verified accuracy once per perceived group/incident, clamps score to [-2,2], and tracks original accuracy, observed corrections/retractions and relay accuracy separately. Retain is a replaceable no-factual-update control. Repeated verification is idempotent; conflicting tokens preserve existing history.

Credibility first applies unchanged 005 provenance/revision standing, then weights each contributing named-source group by max(0,1+score), within 0–3. Opaque groups stay neutral; private anchors keep 2/6 and disputed/retracted/own-source groups stay excluded. Neutral values reproduce 005 support. Credibility is local heuristic reliance, never truth, moral character or a calibrated probability. Stable identities, causal deliveries, explicit bounds, fresh incidents, preserved snapshots and deterministic ordering maintain the Constitution's ten principles while introducing only authorized persistent consequences. Full multi-incident reports remain observer-only.


Experiment 007 is an additive wrapper in `src/experiment007/`. `series.rs` executes the unchanged005 incident engine and006 ingestion/experience listener, with independent fresh temporary policies and each observer's bounded persistent `Memory`. `ListenerFactory`, `VerificationPolicy`, `Predictor`, `WorldGenerator` and `EvaluationPolicy` permit narrow substitutions. Caller-supplied implementations are explicitly marked unversioned; built-ins have separate007 provenance. Custom predictor outputs must preserve the captured input and valid rational probabilities.

`verification.rs` separates token transport authenticity, declared episode/session association, retained local contradiction and acceptance. `VerificationView` contains only the recipient's local memory, delivered token, completed episode and session. Fault source labels, actual actor and factual accuracy live in `FeedbackAudit` only. Authentication is a simulated channel property, not cryptography. `Frozen006` calls the unchanged006 updater after transport mapping. `ConflictAware` validates007 headers, quarantines conflicting retained episode outcomes, then replays only retained experiences in stable order over a compact consequence checkpoint. Recalculation is necessary because clamped scores are nonadditive. On experience eviction, accepted consequences enter the checkpoint; later contradictory evidence cannot reconstruct forgotten detail or undo those consequences. Token journal eviction is separately logged. Bounds are eight experiences, eight retained episode receipts, 32 received token entries and six local credit summaries.

`prediction.rs` receives only source ID and the observing individual's historical credit counters before the final incident. Its Beta(1,1) frequency forecast is evaluated against future assertion correctness by the observer after execution, alongside neutral probability1/2 and a nonprobabilistic ordinal006 score. Forecast probabilities never influence voting. Rational probabilities and deterministic integer Brier/log-loss/calibration arithmetic avoid floating-point platform drift. Independent floating-point references appear only in numerical unit tests.

`generation.rs` defines disjoint development and held-out worlds, permuted identities/orders, source counts, missing histories, correction opportunities, behavior changes and declared verification interventions. Sources speak only to nonsources so matched current testimony stays fixed across listener/acceptance conditions. `evaluation.rs` records compact causal traces and full-report section hashes, separates coverage from conditional accuracy and bootstraps whole matched worlds. Every series is executed twice before inclusion.

`canonical.rs` hashes recursively key-sorted compact JSON, preserving arrays and rejecting floating-point values. It covers full incidents/events, feedback, cognition/memories, forecast inputs and ballots/outcomes, plus evaluation summaries. Execution metadata remains outside simulation hashes. The replay comparator requires distinct Windows/Linux executions with identical revision, toolchain, architecture and locked dependencies. The committed CI matrix can execute both platforms; an unexecuted workflow is not portability evidence. See007 validation for actual platform results.

## Accepted Experiment 011 architecture

The headless 011 module preserves historical engines and introduces a typed scheduler at the existing two discussion opportunities per individual. `model` defines bounded method wires, declared sharing/listening kinds and configuration. `sharing` implements six replaceable `SharingPolicy` baselines and validates delivery/revision/relay legality. `memory` retains at most 12 received and eight own sent wires with explicit FIFO evictions; current branch standing is derived only from retained received relationships. `policy` provides `SocialInterpreter` and the 011 `InvestigationPolicy` boundary. `generation` declares paired worlds, paid diagnostic interventions and independent routing/order draws. `evaluation` scores captured records and produces compact causal traces and complete/section hashes.

Own 010 method memory and own 011 social memory enter `SharingView` alongside legal family descriptors, current local communication information and own 006 credits. A private `SharingChoice` records selected strategy/basis; only a versioned `Message` is delivered. The resolver records selected versus controller origin, legality, recipient effects and audit-only actual transmission ancestry. A report consumes the existing opportunity instead of ordinary incident speech. Legacy silence bookkeeping is accompanied by the typed method decision, including failed depth checks.

`InvestigationView` wraps the narrow 010 personal view plus own social records and declared listener kind. The baseline computes a captured direct-only proposal and then, when enabled, substitutes bounded accuracy/availability expectations in the unchanged 009 information-value selector. Social pseudo-support is capped at four units, kept out of own 010 counts, and never alters prices, finding strength or sealed outputs. Built-in 011 comparisons use 010 ExperienceAdjusted. With social interpretation enabled, the configured base selector supplies the recorded personal proposal, while the final built-in choice uses 009 InformationValue with effective quality; arbitrary base Never/Frozen/exploration behavior is not preserved by that composition. DirectOnly retains the configured base choice. Custom components can implement the narrow 011 investigation interface for other strategies; those combinations were not evaluated here. Actual paid findings and legally accepted fallible 007 feedback update personal history. Interpersonal credit remains a different local mechanism.

DirectOnly ignores reports. Naive uses one latest legitimate branch per immediate speaker/family. ProvenanceAware additionally walks retained received parent links and unverified claimed origins; absent parents form opaque groups rather than being recovered from sent records or audit storage. CredibilityWeighted uses the individual's own credit for the grouped named source (0-3), with neutral weight for opaque groups. Retractions/contests contribute zero; revisions preserve historical wires and original branch association. Separate conditional-accuracy and missingness pseudo-support use Beta(1,1) personal priors and capped weights, never transferable verification claims.

The observer's complete `Report` contains original configuration, objective events, every policy input/action/effect, memory snapshots, costs and outcomes. Compact frozen evidence retains original actions/effects, input hashes and seven full/section digests per series. Exact committed scenario/run regenerations must match those hashes for observer audit; no individual receives the observer archive or repaired forgotten cognition. [Findings](../experiments/011/results.md) record both successful local influence and adverse/null comparisons. No ECS, new dependency, global reputation, truth broadcast, GUI or advanced learning was introduced.


## Experiment 012 implementation boundary

A separate headless `experiment012` engine preserves every historical scheduler. New `model`/`inquiry` modules define typed requests, scoped response envelopes, replaceable requester/responder policies and bounded per-observer inquiry memory. Six local views include only own retained experience, received social reports, own credibility/responsiveness, public accessible method descriptions, remaining effort and remaining speech capacity. Hidden respondent counts, sealed outcomes and actual ancestry remain observer records. `reports` copies only the 011 resolver boundary to extend communication reference slots; old 011 interpretation and support weights are reused unchanged.

The scheduler batches first-phase requests, selects one voluntary response per individual, delivers answers before all investigation selections, then permits final communication and simultaneous votes. At most two submitted outbound actions per individual/incident span all phases; rejected submissions consume a remaining opportunity, silence does not, and exhausted final opportunities record a built-in ordinary-policy counterfactual proposal. Queries, answers, ordinary assertions, method reports and corrections share capacity. Investigation retains independent paid resource accounting and old evidence weights. Late and wider-delivery controls are explicit logged interventions.

Request/response envelopes keep wire content separate from private strategy/basis and controller origin. Received assessments adapt into unchanged 011 social memory; personal 010 verification counts never absorb testimony. Requests and answers have separate bounded FIFO storage and logged evictions, plus retained bounded local responsiveness counts; these measure reply occurrence, not truth or usefulness. Full reports capture views/actions/effects; compact evidence preserves actual choices, local counts, wires, before/after answer forecasts and investigation actions, capacity traces, ordinary messages and eight complete/section digests per series. The scientific boundary and fixed rules are in experiments/012/specification.md.
