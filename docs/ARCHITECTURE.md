# Architecture

Implemented: `src/lib.rs` contains domain types, baseline policies, validation, deterministic PRNG, phase resolver, and append-only reports. `src/main.rs` provides single-run and evaluation CLI modes.

Engine truth lives in Report/objective events and is never passed to Policy. AgentView owns only ID, legal roster, phase, remaining opportunities, local observation/belief, and public claims. Listener claims contain no belief, intent, observation provenance, or objective truth. Decision audit snapshots include policy input, action, communication intent, and acceptance; resolver events record effects. Policies receive no audit reference. A caller implementing a custom policy must not inject external truth; the interface cannot prevent malicious host-side side channels.

Ballots are collected without publishing them until all decisions finish. No mutable report is returned during execution. Serialization contains no clocks, hash iteration, or platform paths. Full reports intentionally expose observer secrets and must not be fed back to agents.

Experiment 002 adds `src/experiment002.rs` and a separate `experiment002` CLI. It reuses only the phase type, six-person constant, deterministic PRNG, and majority tally function; 001 policies, inputs, serialization and resolver remain unchanged. Its independently versioned Config contains per-person private incentives and the controller briefing toggle. IndividualState separates original observation, participation briefing, candidate belief, and incentives. Its AgentView contains one own state and public Message values only. Policy has no access to Config, another individual's state, actor assignment, audit, classifications, scores of other speakers, or prior ballots.

Choice records intent, option scores, and mechanism at decision time. Baselines retain only a private `spoken` flag (logged through the budget-used mechanism and preceding decisions); all other inputs are explicit in the view. The engine independently classifies the delivered action against the recorded local belief and world actor. Unsubstantiated intent labels are retained as such. Public uncertainty is a message without an asserted candidate. Custom policies are labeled unversioned and require caller-managed provenance for reproducibility. Separate finite local state and append-only observer records preserve historical observations even when the initial briefing supplies a different belief. See experiments/002/specification.md for formulas and fixed assumptions.

Experiment 003 lives in `src/experiment003/`: belief.rs defines local support state and replaceable Listener, engine.rs defines validated configs, delivery, snapshots, CommunicationPolicy and JudgmentPolicy, and evaluation.rs defines preregistered fixtures and paired measurements. A separate experiment003 CLI leaves historical commands and serialization intact. The 002 communication interface is adapted using only original own state, current candidate, legal roster/phase/budget and received Message values. Judgment is independently selected; silent communication no longer implicitly controls voting in this experiment.

ListenerView contains only ID, original own state, prior support state and legitimately received messages. Support is recomputed from original evidence and one assertion per other speaker, not accumulated from old scores. Observer Update records attach factual correctness after assessment; those fields are outside the listener input. Decision references its preceding update. Testimony records attach source belief, intent, classification, recipients and origin for auditing; the delivered Message has none of these private/audit fields. Explicit Assigned, Injected, Delivered, Updated and Resolved events support chronological trace checks. History retains old observations and mental snapshots even when current beliefs change.

Config 003 explicitly declares ID mapping, speaking order, observation overrides and injected research testimony. Config validation checks versions, legal IDs, both permutations, recipient uniqueness and a 12-message external intervention cap. Individual communication remains capped at 12 opportunities and ballots at six. Injections consume the separate declared controller allowance; selected and injected messages remain distinguishable. No engine truth, sender intentions, speaker beliefs, other private states, common-source provenance, audit or experiment controls enter ordinary listener views. Full reports remain observer-only. The implementation is headless and uses no new dependencies.

Experiment 004 lives in `src/experiment004/`: model.rs defines local interfaces, wire attribution and baseline/relay adapters; listener.rs implements visible source grouping; engine.rs validates delivery/basis and stores audit lineage; evaluation.rs defines the preregistered comparisons. Its CLI is experiment004. Historical engines and serialized evidence retain their own versions.

Message exposes only ID, immediate speaker, proposition and claimed attribution. AgentView adds only original own state, revised belief, received messages and permitted recipients to the legal roster/phase/budget. ListenerView has only original own state, prior belief and received messages. Neither receives Testimony, Config, actual roots, another private observation, chosen sender strategy, classifications or observer truth. The private action Basis and Strategy enter the resolver/audit, never ordinary dialogue.

SourceCount strips attribution and calls the unchanged 003 listener. Provenance resolves earlier matching references within received storage only, capped at three links. Missing parents create opaque groups; named and firsthand origins remain unverified. Assessments preserve group choice, checked visible IDs, uncertainty and duplicate/own-source discounts; origin_verified remains false. Support is recomputed from original anchors, with one point per first declared group and first assertion per speaker. Later corrections remain ignored.

Observer Lineage separately retains validated private acquisition or exact received-copy basis, actual parent message IDs, sorted acquisition/construction roots and bounded depth. Inference roots use recorded candidate-support assessments. Decision-time choices and listener snapshots precede effects; delivery events enumerate actual recipients. Selected origins reference decisions; injected origins reference controller interventions. Acquisitions measure separate assignments, not independent real-world witnesses. Report storage never becomes an agent memory lookup.

Config declares scoped routes, order, original observations, separate listener/communication/judgment selections and injections. Validation bounds discussion opportunities, controller messages, IDs, routes, source references, exact copies and causal depth. Each individual retains only its delivered messages for the finite game; no eviction or long-term storage mechanism is introduced. Policies and listeners are replaceable, while custom callers remain responsible for external side channels and implementation provenance. Serialized size metrics quantify message copies and audit values without claiming RAM or timing measurements.

Experiment 005 lives in `src/experiment005/`, with model, listener, engine and evaluation modules plus its separate CLI. Message carries Assert/Uncertain or Revise {own earlier target, optional replacement, optional forwarded message reference}, immediate speaker and claimed attribution. ListenerView contains only original own state, received messages, own observation history and prior belief. AgentView adds legitimate own sent records, current belief, opportunities, permitted recipients and local correction costs. Policies never receive Report, Config, another individual's observations, intent labels, actual roots or correctness flags.

Later observations are scoped controller deliveries before declared decision slots; each gets an immutable acquisition record. The latest own observation supersedes the weight-2 private anchor without editing older observations; an explicit participation briefing retains weight-6 precedence. Both listener variants share this new private-input mechanism. Frozen004 projects only original assertions/uncertainty into the unchanged 004 listener and records typed revisions as ignored. Historical 004 execution and evidence remain untouched.

The revision listener builds visible own-speaker branches through explicit earlier target links, computes current leaves, and records standing separately from immutable Message. Competing replacements dispute a branch; a retraction supplies no replacement. Valid revisions retain their initial perceived group. Conflicting active members dispute a group rather than increase corroboration. A speaker cannot revise another speaker's source statement or stale relay. Missing original/forwarded messages remain missing; nothing reads the audit to repair access. Claimed associations and copied message relationships never verify truth or actual origin.

Sincere/Selective/Misleading policies may use a second communication opportunity for one revision; Retain keeps frozen once-spoken behavior. RelayRevision can forward an exact legitimately received revision by revising its own earlier relay, subject to current candidate agreement and local cost. Choice snapshots record recognized mismatch, available forwarding reference, scores and intent independently from actual selected disclosure. Separate replaceable judgments execute ballots.

Observer Lineage distinguishes evidence parents/roots from revision_of historical dependency; replacement evidence does not inherit the old assertion as support merely because it targets it. Evidence depth is capped at three. Testimony attaches intent, source belief, classification and correctness outside wire content. Controller injections carry distinct origins and preserved resolver input snapshots, while selected messages reference actual decisions. All assignments, observations, updates, decisions, deliveries, resolutions and outcomes are appended. Config caps injections and later observations at 12 each, retains 12 individual communication opportunities and six ballots, and versions two scoped route tables. Finite received and sent histories have no evictions. No dependencies, ECS, reputation or long-term memory were introduced.
