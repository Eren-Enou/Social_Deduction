# Roadmap

Completed Experiment 001: rules, isolation, voluntary structured communication, audit, reproducibility; preserved frozen baseline.

Completed Experiment 002: legitimate participation briefing, private incentive weights, explicit truthful/silent/deceptive controls, incentive-sensitive voluntary selection, mistaken-vs-contradictory classification, matched interventions, and retained negative speech effects. See experiments/002/results.md.

Completed Experiment 003: local evidence scoring, belief revision/retention, separate current-belief judgment, traceable delivery/update/action chains, truthful/mistaken/deceptive influence, counterfactual and hidden-intention controls, ID mapping and speaking-order evaluations. See experiments/003/results.md and validation.md.

Completed Experiment 004: typed claimed attribution, scoped delivery, voluntary exact-copy relays, separate observer acquisition chains and recipient-visible groups, bounded local reference traversal, source-count comparisons, hidden-origin controls, inaccurate attribution and negative judgments. See experiments/004/results.md and validation.md. Provenance sometimes reduced duplicate contributions, but did not reliably improve verdicts or reveal hidden dependence.

Completed Experiment 005: immutable own-message corrections/retractions, scoped later observations, voluntary correction/withholding costs, faithful correction forwarding, visible branch standing, disputed provenance groups and measured belief/vote recovery. Preserved misleading and sincerely wrong revisions, missing-reference failures, stale relays and injected-control distinctions. See experiments/005/results.md and validation.md.

Proposed later questions: alternative revision acceptance/forwarding rules, provenance repair, broader order/source structures, weights and richer voluntary communication. Persistent trust, reputation, long-term memory and learning remain deferred until their mechanisms and evaluations are separately authorized and preregistered. No Experiment 006 has begun.
