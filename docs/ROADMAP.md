# Roadmap

Completed Experiment 001: rules, isolation, voluntary structured communication, audit, reproducibility; preserved frozen baseline.

Completed Experiment 002: legitimate participation briefing, private incentive weights, explicit truthful/silent/deceptive controls, incentive-sensitive voluntary selection, mistaken-vs-contradictory classification, matched interventions, and retained negative speech effects. See experiments/002/results.md.

Completed Experiment 003: local evidence scoring, belief revision/retention, separate current-belief judgment, traceable delivery/update/action chains, truthful/mistaken/deceptive influence, counterfactual and hidden-intention controls, ID mapping and speaking-order evaluations. See experiments/003/results.md and validation.md.

Completed Experiment 004: typed claimed attribution, scoped delivery, voluntary exact-copy relays, separate observer acquisition chains and recipient-visible groups, bounded local reference traversal, source-count comparisons, hidden-origin controls, inaccurate attribution and negative judgments. See experiments/004/results.md and validation.md. Provenance sometimes reduced duplicate contributions, but did not reliably improve verdicts or reveal hidden dependence.

Completed Experiment 005: immutable own-message corrections/retractions, scoped later observations, voluntary correction/withholding costs, faithful correction forwarding, visible branch standing, disputed provenance groups and measured belief/vote recovery. Preserved misleading and sincerely wrong revisions, missing-reference failures, stale relays and injected-control distinctions. See experiments/005/results.md and validation.md.

Completed Experiment 006: stable identities across bounded independent incidents; received-only FIFO experience; scoped authenticated laboratory verification; separate original/final accuracy, corrections and relay responsibility; reversible local credit and credibility-weighted005 interpretation. Evaluated 352 reproducible series/1056 incidents. Different legitimate histories change later ballots, and both beneficial and adverse judgment effects are preserved. See experiments/006/results.md and validation.md. The mechanism is a transparent programmed heuristic, not calibrated trust or advanced social learning.

Proposed later questions: alternative revision acceptance/forwarding rules, provenance repair, broader order/source structures, update/retention/weight rules, realistic outcome verification and richer voluntary communication. Global reputation, coalitions, trained models and broader learning remain deferred pending authorization and separate preregistration. Experiment007 was subsequently authorized; see the separate stage below.


Completed Experiment007 engineering implementation: versioned verification faults, unchanged006 acceptance baseline, bounded local conflict quarantine/recomputation, separate neutral/ordinal/Beta(1,1) forecasts, disjoint generated-world evaluation, coverage/conditional-accuracy/utility comparisons and complete canonical manifests. Findings retain corrupted credibility, predictive and judgment failures. Actual Windows replay is evaluated; Linux and cross-platform agreement remain unverified because no local Linux execution environment is available and CI has not run. See experiments/007/results.md and validation.md. No Experiment008 has begun; new mechanisms require separate authorization and preregistration.

Experiment008 is separately authorized and preregistered in `eea133f`: optional costly investigation, latent fallible environmental evidence, conserved local effort, private finding disclosure, visible-source deduplication and paired held-out acquisition evaluation. See experiments/008/specification.md for fixed hypotheses and controls. Implementation/evaluation status and factual limitations are recorded in experiments/008/results.md and validation.md. Investigation heuristics are programmed, not trained social intelligence. Experiment009 remains outside scope; future proposals require separate authorization and preregistration.
