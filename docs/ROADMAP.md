Completed Experiment 006: stable identities across bounded independent incidents; received-only FIFO experience; scoped authenticated laboratory verification; separate original/final accuracy, corrections and relay responsibility; reversible local credit and credibility-weighted005 interpretation. Evaluated 352 reproducible series/1056 incidents. Different legitimate histories change later ballots, and both beneficial and adverse judgment effects are preserved. See experiments/006/results.md and validation.md. The mechanism is a transparent programmed heuristic, not calibrated trust or advanced social learning.

Proposed later questions: alternative revision acceptance/forwarding rules, provenance repair, broader order/source structures, update/retention/weight rules, realistic outcome verification and richer voluntary communication. Global reputation, coalitions, trained models and broader learning remain deferred pending authorization and separate preregistration. Experiment007 was subsequently authorized; see the separate stage below.
