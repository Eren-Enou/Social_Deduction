# Simulation Constitution

Authoritative for Experiments 001 through 007. Reconstructed from the supplied AGENTS.md because the referenced documents were absent at development start. Its ten principles are unchanged by subsequent experiments. Public records mean legitimately delivered messages: scoped communications remain unavailable to other individuals. In 005, revisions change current evidential interpretation, never historical message existence or old mental snapshots. In 006, only declared local memories/consequences persist; authenticated outcome feedback is explicitly delivered controller evidence, and bounded detail evictions cannot be repaired from the observer archive. In 007, authentication and factual accuracy are distinct; retained contradiction permits local quarantine, while evicted detail cannot be reconstructed. Each replaceable cognition component receives its narrow local view.

1. Identifiable causality: record inputs, actions, validation, and effects.
2. Information locality: policies receive only typed legal observations and public records.
3. Persistent consequences: accepted claims remain public and votes determine the final result.
4. Individual perspective: objective truth, beliefs, claims, and intent are separate.
5. Resource consistency: finite discussion opportunities and one ballot per individual.
6. Reproducibility: versioned configuration, stable ordering, deterministic random streams.
7. No predetermined narrative outcomes: silence, abstention, and unresolved judgments are legal.
8. Historical traceability: append-only records and decision-time snapshots.
9. Replaceable policies: policy interface accepts AgentView only.
10. Measured complexity: no ECS, learning, reputation, or bounded memory without evidence of need.
