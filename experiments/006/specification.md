multi-incident-006-v1, config-006-v1, local-memory-006-v1, accuracy-update-006-v1, lab-verification-006-v1, credibility-listener-006-v1. Reuse unchanged 005 rules/resolver/revision listener and communication/judgment policies via replaceable listener adapters. Six persistent IDs 0–5, at most eight independent incidents, 18 decisions each, fresh temporary state/policies per incident. Only declared local credit summaries and bounded experience records persist. Message/acquisition/event indices remain incident-local inside an episode envelope; cross-incident references always pair episode and local ID. No previous game or observer report is policy input.

H1: legitimate verified experience changes source reliance and may change later ballots under identical present observations/messages/rules. H2: undelivered or unverified feedback cannot change factual credit; identical experienced histories yield identical local assessments despite different hidden history or intervention intent. H3: observers with different deliveries can disagree about one source. H4: truthful history can help on truthful future testimony but amplify later falsehood, while earlier errors can suppress a later correct report. H5: contradictory later verified experiences can reverse a positive score. H6: visible relays/corrections retain source grouping; neither repetition nor correction choice alone adds independent factual credit. No accuracy optimization or primary weight tuning.

## Feedback integrity and local memory

Controller feedback plans specify after_episode, about_episode <= after_episode, selected recipient IDs and kind. At most 24 plans per series. Verification delivers {episode,actual actor,issuer lab-verification-006-v1} derived once from the completed incident. Its integrity model is a trusted experimental controller authenticating an exact outcome, not natural inference, a signature implementation or a simulated institutional discovery. Individual updaters receive the delivered token only, never a report/reference to hidden truth. Verdict announcements deliver the collective candidate (possibly wrong); participant outcome claims deliver a declared candidate. Neither is authenticated and neither updates factual credibility.

Each local memory starts neutral: six summaries with score 0 and zero verified counts. FIFO capacity eight experience records per individual; each record contains namespaced received message summaries (max 24), perceived group, received original source if identifiable, initial and final candidate standing, observed revisions/retractions, relay participants, verification status and unverified announcement state. Ingest only the individual's final received messages and frozen 005 visible interpretation, never Testimony's actual roots, intent, classifications or another individual's state. Store original-source responsibility only when the group names a speaker whose own original assertion was actually received; otherwise unknown. A reported named source without that person's report or an opaque missing parent cannot acquire factual credit through hidden lineage.

One record per perceived group per incident prevents relay/copy multiplication. The source's own received branch supplies its final standing; relay accuracy is recorded separately as observed copied reporting, not evidence that every relay originated an error or deliberately deceived. Retraction or disputed/malformed source branch has no final assertion to grade. At ingestion, observed own corrections are counted separately from verified accuracy. Keep a bounded count of relay participants and their final report candidates. No retained source gains credit because an unseen report happens to exist in the observer archive.

FIFO evictions are logged before future feedback. Factual/correction summaries are separately retained consequences and remain bounded even after detailed records leave memory. Never reconstruct evicted claims using an audit archive; late feedback for an evicted/never-experienced incident has no eligible record. Repeated verification of a retained record is idempotent. A conflicting purported authenticated token is rejected as an integrity conflict, not silently substituted. Unverified claims may update the record's announcement field but never its score. Unknown/unverified experiences stay explicitly unknown. Scores are heuristic reliance weights, not calibrated probabilities or moral traits.

## Exact update and weighting rules

Evidence updater: for each retained, previously unverified, eligible received original-source record covered by the delivered verified outcome, grade initial and final assertions separately. Initial correctness increments a separate original-accurate/original-inaccurate counter. Final candidate correct adds +1 to score; incorrect adds -1; no final candidate adds 0. Clamp score to [-2,2]. Increment verified-final-accurate/inaccurate counts only for a known final candidate. Other counters saturate u16. Observed correction/retraction counts are separate; verified final accuracy, not the act of correcting, changes score. A repair may therefore gain final-accuracy credit despite an initially wrong report, while a still-wrong correction loses credit and a retraction gains none. Explicitly preserve this assumption and adverse cases.

Updater Retain is a replaceable negative control: record experiences/feedback statuses without changing factual summaries. All primary comparisons use Evidence. Final accuracy deduplicates one source/group/episode. Grade received relay branches separately into relay-accurate/relay-inaccurate counters, never the relay's original-source score solely for an explicit visible relay. Misattribution remains an unverified association; hidden actual origins cannot repair it.

Credibility listener first calls unchanged 005 Revision to establish standing/provenance/contests. Keep private weights 2/6, zero for retracted/disputed groups and returned own-source echoes. For each otherwise contributing group: named speaker score s gives weight max(0,1+s), bounded 0–3; opaque groups have neutral weight 1. One weight per perceived group, not per message/speaker. Neutral score 0 exactly matches 005 support. Negative scores may suppress useful testimony; positive scores may amplify errors or misattributed claims. Unique positive maximum and legal abstention are unchanged. Frozen005 baseline calls the unchanged 005 Revision listener with weight 1, while executing the same local experience/feedback updater; only use of credit differs.

Each callback records the exact credit input and weighting result alongside the corresponding unchanged engine Update input/output; it is not a later policy rerun. Per-episode memory-before/after, ingestion and feedback input/output/eviction records preserve causal ancestry. Agent policies see current belief; persistent memory influences communication only through this listener input in the initial implementation. Listener adapters see one observer's bounded summaries, never other memories. Processing counts are deterministic record inspections/group weight operations; no runtime/RAM benchmark claim.

## Fixtures and comparisons specified before implementation

22 series fixtures × two listeners (Frozen005, Credibility) × base seeds 0–7 = 352 series. Episode seed = base_seed*16 + episode index, chosen before evaluation. Stable source A=0 and competing source B=2, unless explicitly permuted. Four final recipients 1/3/4/5. History incidents normally have only selected truthful-to-local-belief source A, privately observing true actor for accurate histories or next candidate for inaccurate histories, delivered to all. Feedback normally verifies to all six after each history. Current assessment incident has A asserting actual actor and B independently asserting next candidate, with neither speaker receiving the other's message; recipients have no private actor observation, silent communication and current judgment. Equal neutral contributions tie; history can break the tie. No verification of the current assessment is scheduled.

1. Neutral: current incident only, empty histories.
2. Accurate: two accurate verified history incidents, then current.
3. Inaccurate: one inaccurate verified history, then current (formerly inaccurate A now correct).
4. DifferentHistories: accurate history verifies only to3; inaccurate history verifies only to5; same current messages to both.
5. MissingFeedback: two accurate histories, no verification, then current.
6. WrongVerdict: one inaccurate history yields wrong collective verdict, announced without verification, then current.
7. Corrected: inaccurate original followed by legitimate new private actor observation and selected sincere repair (005 two rounds), verified after history, then current.
8. Uncorrected: same later private evidence but Retain policy withholds correction, verified after history, then current.
9. MisleadingCorrection: wrong original, true later private actor observation, Misleading policy replaces with actor+2, verified incorrect, then current.
10. RelayOrigin: false A original plus two selected disclosed relays, all recipient messages and verifier available, then current. One source experience; relay reporting tracked separately.
11. OpaqueRelay: false A only to0/1/2; two relays to3/4/5 cite missing A; verification available, then current. Final recipients have no received original-source branch.
12. Misattributed: same hidden A delivery, relays name3 instead; verifier available but 3 never supplied an original assertion. No hidden responsibility assignment.
13. SelectiveFeedback: two accurate histories verify only to3, two inaccurate histories only to5; then current. Partial experience can bias local trust.
14. Reversal: two accurate histories followed by three inaccurate verified histories, then current. Record score path, not just endpoints.
15. IdentityPermuted: Accurate with source A=3/B=0 and observer roles accordingly; current actor proposition/seed unchanged. This permutes speaker roles, not the world's candidate labels.
16. OrderReversed: Accurate with speaking order reversed in each incident independently of source identity.
17. InjectedExperience: Accurate histories use explicit controller assertions matching the selected history wire content; current remains selected. Compare equal local credit/decisions without using intervention origin.
18. TrustedFalse: Accurate histories, then current A asserts wrong actor+1 and B actual actor; tests trusted source still misleading recipients.
19. DelayedFeedback: two accurate histories; verify episode0 and1 only after episode1. Later feedback updates retained pending records before current.
20. Eviction: seven history incidents; first accurate, remaining inaccurate, with verification of each except first; late first-incident verification after the current eighth incident. Current two source records push total over FIFO capacity; evicted first record cannot be reconstructed.
21. Interaction: Accurate histories; current competing B also receives A's current message before its truthful policy turn. Credibility may alter B's chosen testimony. Analyze separately from fixed-message comparison.
22. UnverifiedClaim: two accurate histories with participant outcome claims naming a wrong candidate, no authenticated verification, then current.

Total 66 incidents across one seed/listener fixture set, hence 1056 executed incidents in the primary 352 series; every series is replayed. Within-fixture pairs vary only listener use; verify identical current initial configuration and delivered wire/recipients except possible Interaction. Compare Accurate with Neutral/MissingFeedback, Inaccurate, TrustedFalse, DelayedFeedback, IdentityPermuted, OrderReversed, InjectedExperience; Corrected with Uncorrected/MisleadingCorrection; DifferentHistories with SelectiveFeedback; RelayOrigin with OpaqueRelay/Misattributed. Declare differing histories/episode counts/seed indices rather than pretending all between-fixture currents are identical. Within-fixture enablement is the primary matched present-incident control. Additional engineering negative controls perturb hidden history/intent with fixed local deliveries.
