## Verification and recipient boundary

Token fields: version, claimed issuer, simulated authenticated-channel signal, session, episode, serial, kind and candidate. Known issuer is lab-verification-006-v1. Authenticated channel is a declared simulated transport integrity signal, not cryptography. Ordinary verdict announcements are a different kind. Authenticity says recognized issuer plus valid channel, never factual accuracy. Full observer audit separately identifies actual source/intervention, scheduling/recipient, actual about-episode/actor, authenticity and factual relationship. The wire has no actual actor, corruption flag, private intent or hidden report reference.

007 association checks require token-007-v1, matching public series session and serial/4 == claimed episode (four issuance positions per incident). A malformed binding/session cannot attach to a different local experience. Unknown/future/inaccessible/evicted episodes leave a pending no-eligible-experience result, never reconstruct from audit. Token serials are simulated protocol metadata, not secure signatures or evidence that an issuer is competent. An authenticated outcome using a valid binding may still be wrong; a wrongly issued but internally consistent association cannot be detected through objective truth.

Frozen006 acceptance translates legitimate channel feedback into unchanged006 Evidence updater fields and preserves first-authenticated-outcome retention, duplicate idempotence and ignored contradictions. Invalid transport maps to an unrecognized issuer. It ignores new session/serial binding checks, reflecting the old interface's absence of these fields; do not call it a historical006 serialization change. Unverified verdicts translate to unchanged unauthenticated announcements. ConflictAware additionally checks version/session/serial, tracks accepted outcomes for retained episodes, repeats same candidate without adding evidence, and quarantines an episode upon a second incompatible authenticated candidate. Quarantine is sticky for that retained episode, regardless of later agreement or which outcome is objectively correct. It never assumes the newest token is true. Pending deliveries are not automatically attached to subsequently acquired memories; a new delivery is needed.

ConflictAware recomputes factual scores/counts from a bounded consequence checkpoint plus currently retained eligible accepted records, folded in experience episode/group order. Each record retains immutable received claims/standing; disputed verification contributes zero factual accuracy while observed correction/retraction behavior remains. Apply006 original/final accuracy and relay-responsibility rules, score ±1 clamped [-2,2], counters saturated u16. At FIFO eviction, fold any still-accepted contribution into the checkpoint; that compact consequence survives without its detailed claim. A later conflict for an entirely evicted experience cannot undo that consequence. Never fetch prior audit snapshots. This chronological retained-record recomputation is a declared007 update mechanism; delayed-order effects can differ from006's delivery-order accumulation. Historical decisions/feedback snapshots remain immutable.

## Predictions and calibration

Forecast one target observer's next first original assertion from each source before the current incident's messages arrive. Target is the final nonsource identity in the generated permutation. Predictor input is only own source score and legitimately accumulated accurate/inaccurate final counts. Source selection is a declared evaluation target; forecasts see neither future proposition nor objective correctness. Score forecasts only subsequently executed first assertions; future outcomes are attached by evaluator after execution, never during prediction. Target event is factual accuracy of that future source assertion, not honesty/intent/confidence.

Neutral probability is exactly1/2, an analytic prior from symmetric high/low source generation; no empirical fitting or held-out rate estimation.006 ordinal predictor retains score as a category: positive predicts accurate, negative inaccurate, zero unknown; report categorical coverage/accuracy by score and experience count, never label scores probabilities. Independent research probability is Beta(1,1) smoothing: (accurate+1)/(accurate+inaccurate+2), represented as exact numerator/denominator. It is not used in voting. Counts affected by accepted false verification remain locally legitimate but factually contaminated.

Brier loss rounds each rational squared error to integer units of1e-6. Log loss clips probability to [1/1024,1023/1024] and uses deterministic fixed-point natural log: scale1e15, power-of-two range reduction to [1/2,1], 32 terms of the atanh expansion and fixed ln2=693147180559945/1e15, then truncate to1e-6 units. No floating point in evidence. Five equal-width reliability bins [0,.2),[.2,.4),[.4,.6),[.6,.8),[.8,1], each showing sample count, forecast sum and factual successes. ECE is sum absolute within-bin forecast-sum minus successes, divided by total samples, in1e-6 units. Show Brier/log loss/ordinal results conditional on verified-count category0,1,2,3,4+ and fault/source-regime. Empty cells remain absent/undefined, not perfect. Numerical tests compare known losses and exact bin boundaries. Calibration improvement requires better held-out Brier plus meaningful reliability diagnostics; a narrow bootstrap interval or a numerical probability alone is not proof of broad calibrated trust.

## Generated held-out worlds

Development seeds0–15, held-out seeds1000–1063 (64 distinct world units), deterministic existing SplitMix64-v1 with generator seed=world seed. No parameter estimation/tuning. Generation draws, in this order: Fisher-Yates identity permutation, independent speaking-order permutation, source count1–3, history count0–4, stable versus changed behavior bit, source accuracy thresholds1 or3 out of4 independently, historical correction toggle. For each incident draw its seed from the generator; true actor uses unchanged005 mapping. For each historical source draw missing observation with probability1/4; otherwise accuracy by its threshold/4 and incorrect candidate offset uniformly1–5. With correction toggle, deliver true later observation and choose Sincere for history sources, recording selected repair versus no report. Current sources always receive a candidate: threshold/4 accuracy in stable worlds and (4-threshold)/4 in changed worlds, incorrect offset uniformly1–5. Nonsource histories have no observation; current nonsources except target independently get an observation with probability1/4, correct with probability1/2 and otherwise uniform incorrect offset. These assigned inaccuracies are mistakes, not forced contrary intent.

Sources report through unchanged truthful/Sincere policies to nonsources only; they never receive another source's report. Nonsources remain voluntarily silent with current-belief voting. Thus current delivered testimony stays fixed across comparison policies, while observations, source composition, actor/identities/order, histories, corrections and source drift vary across worlds. Use six verification streams for every held-out world: Clean, AuthenticatedFalse (next actor), UnauthenticatedFalse, Conflict (accurate then incompatible issued result), Missing, Selective (only first nonsource). All except Missing deliver after each history; Conflict issues two distinct serials. Current assessment is never verified. Pair two acceptance policies × two listeners (Frozen005 neutral versus unchanged006 Credibility) for each stream. Total1536 generated series; no pooling repeated streams/policies as independent worlds.

Sixteen diagnostic families × seeds0–3 × two acceptance × two listeners =256 additional series: Accurate, RewardFalse, PenalizeTrue, Unauthenticated, ConflictTrueFirst, ConflictFalseFirst, DelayedContradiction, Missing, Selective, Duplicate, Repeated, ReplayWrongSession, UnknownEpisode, Misassociated, EvictedConflict, Verdict. Reuse006 accurate/inaccurate/eviction initial incidents where appropriate, with explicit007 streams. RewardFalse rewards an inaccurate original and later inaccurate testimony; PenalizeTrue punishes accurate history and then suppresses a correct report. DelayedContradiction includes an assessment before the contradiction and another afterward, preserving the former. EvictedConflict delivers a contradiction after the eight-record FIFO has evicted the targeted original. These are diagnostic interventions, not generalization samples or voluntary verifier behavior.

Total1792 series, each replayed; incident total is derived from generated configuration rather than invented in advance. Primary calibration uses generated Credibility rows per acceptance/fault, not duplicate neutral-listener forecasts. Full row forecasts/labels, source regimes, local verification traces, weighting, ballots/outcomes and per-section canonical digests are retained. Primary comparisons include listener pairs, acceptance pairs and Clean-versus-corrupted streams within identical worlds. Confirm config/wire/private-input equality rather than assuming it.
