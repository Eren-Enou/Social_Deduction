# Experiment 008 findings

Completed headless implementation and measured evaluation on Windows, 2026-10-09. The preregistration was committed as `eea133f` before implementation and primary evaluation. These are programmed acquisition/evidence heuristics, not learned purpose, calibrated trust or emergent social intelligence. No policy was tuned on the held-out outcomes. Linux replay remains unverified.

## Evidence and independent units

Primary evidence contains 608 series/1824 incidents with full deterministic replay: 384 diagnostic series (16 families, four seeds, six labels) and 224 generated series (32 held-out worlds, six investigation policies plus an injected-information control). The 32 generated worlds, seeds 2000-2031, are independent inference units; individual votes and repeated treatments are dependent. Diagnostics are mechanism controls, not generalization samples. The 24 Injected diagnostic series contain six identical controller configurations per seed under different labels; they are declared duplicate controls, never additional independent worlds. A separate cost 1/cost 3 mechanism control contains two matched series/six incidents at seed 0; it is outside the primary grid and held-out inference.

[Frozen primary evidence](../../evidence/experiment-008.json), [actual Windows manifest](../../evidence/experiment-008-windows.json), and [secondary cost control](../../evidence/experiment-008-cost-control.json) preserve adverse and null outcomes. Each primary row retains investigation inputs/assessments/choices, delivery before/after beliefs and balances, public reports, immutable testimony, ancestry and ballots. Six canonical section hashes include the entire full observer report. Regenerate snapshots with `cargo run --locked --release --bin experiment008 -- scenario diagnostic Family seed Policy new-config.json`, or `scenario generated selected|injected seed Policy new-config.json`, then `run new-config.json new-report.json`. Artifact content, truth, private intent and audit roots are excluded from individual decision interfaces.

## Answers to the fifteen research questions

1. **Voluntary response to legitimate uncertainty:** yes under the declared rules. The engine offers an opportunity; it does not require inspection. Primary runs contain 2652 selected inspections and 8292 declines across 10944 opportunities, with zero rejected investigations. ConflictDriven selected 109/576 held-out opportunities; CostSensitive 70, CredibilityAware 90, Always 217 and Random 139. Captured local U/C/warning values explain selection. Engineering tests vary uncertainty while holding other inputs fixed. This demonstrates a coded local mechanism, not learned curiosity.

2. **Scarcity:** yes. Last-token cost increases by 1, and strict ties decline. Budgets are never negative or replenished: 6336 initial primary tokens =2652 spent +3684 remaining. Held-out CostSensitive retains 218/288 tokens, against Always 71/288. The matched secondary cost control spends 10 tokens at declared action cost 1 and zero at 3; correct ballots are 16 versus 13 out of 18. Declared action cost is a policy heuristic; actual inspections still burn one token. This one diagnostic is not held-out reliability evidence.

3. **Selected action versus injected information:** separate origin tags and resource effects are retained. Primary controller delivery count 735 includes 432 duplicated diagnostic-control deliveries and 303 generated-control deliveries; these cost zero. The held-out injected control obtains 42 additional correct ballots, with 18/4/10 improved/worse/unchanged worlds, while selecting no investigation. Its benefit demonstrates information effects, never initiative. An engineering test holds the delivered finding and assessment identical while selected acquisition burns a token and intervention does not.

4. **Beliefs, communication and executed votes:** yes, with qualifications. Of 2652 selected acquisitions, local correct-candidate status improves 1104, worsens 41 and remains unchanged 1507. These are fixed-local-input before/after comparisons, not independent samples or full-game policy effects. All selected/controller deliveries together change candidate 1884 times and support 2964 times. Changed message streams and actual ballots are reported per matched world above.428 informative acquisitions remain undisclosed with no accepted subsequent speech by the investigator. Ordinary disclosure, revision, silence and abstention remain legal; findings are never automatically broadcast.

5. **How often factual accuracy improved:** the paired 32-world counts above answer full-policy effects. Among 625 held-out selected acquisitions across the five active policies, 117 improve local correct-candidate status. These are repeated observations in matched worlds, not 625 independent trials. Always gains 37 correct ballots against Never; ConflictDriven 43; CostSensitive/CredibilityAware 37; Random 39.

6. **Worse or unchanged judgment:**15/625 held-out selected acquisitions worsen local correctness and 493 are unchanged. Primary selected findings comprise 2014 correct, 343 wrong and 295 missing; there is no accuracy flag available to the recipient. The Misleading diagnostic (four seeds, 72 ballots per policy) changes Never's 52 correct/4 wrong/16 abstentions to Always's 4/44/24 at 48 tokens. Missing yields unchanged 52/4/16 while Always pays 48 tokens.349 selected primary acquisitions have no belief/support effect; this is a no-effect proxy, not proof that the decision was irrational.

7. **Inaccurate prior reputation:** positive history can suppress useful investigation. TrustedWrong and ChangedBehavior each leave CredibilityAware at 48 correct/24 wrong out of 72 ballots, zero inspections, including all 24 wrong final-episode ballots. Seed 0 observer 5 sees source 0 credit +2, U0, C1 and declines, then votes 5 when actor 4 caused the incident. With negative history, UnreliableCorrect produces 40 inspections and 44 correct/28 wrong/0 abstentions for CredibilityAware, versus Never 4/28/40. At seed 0 final episode, source 0 credit -2 triggers U5/C2 and a correct vote 4, although that source is now correct. It can spend resources to recover information already present in disregarded testimony. CredibilityAware spends 20 more held-out tokens than CostSensitive for the same 361 correct ballots; benefit from its warning feature is not established generally. A matched executed-history test separately demonstrates H6 without inventing initial reputation from observer labels.

8. **Visible dependence:** the max-weight group rule prevents an extra independent group. A tested source assertion 1 plus known derived finding 2 gives 2, not 3; a received report and multiple inspectors/relays of one artifact count at most one maximum contribution. Across primary acquisitions 237 have disclosed dependence; duplicate/group counters record 16787 duplicate assessment exposures and 17 contested group exposures. These are repeated interpretations, not unique artifacts. Known-dependence strengthening from 1 to 2 is explicitly a weight replacement. It does not guarantee better decisions.

9. **Hidden dependence:** inaccessible.241 acquired records have observer-known but undisclosed dependence. The same tested source assertion 1 plus a hidden dependent artifact 2 gives 3 in two perceived groups; the policy cannot inspect the true root. Equal-view tests change objective actor/content/root while preserving local selection inputs and obtain identical investigation decisions. No hidden independence detector is claimed.

10. **Conservation and later decisions:** yes, as a local-threshold consequence. Conservation/Exhaustion are identical matched-world families under this minimal generator, not separate independent evidence. Across four seeds, Always spends 24 tokens early and yields 52 correct/4 wrong/16 abstentions. ConflictDriven saves the early opportunities, spends 4 tokens later, and yields 72/0/0; Never and CostSensitive also yield 72/0/0 with zero tokens. Thus the experiment demonstrates timing and downstream differences but does not establish an extra accuracy benefit from the later saved-token inspection over Never. In seed 0, observer 1 declines in episode 0, inspects in episode 1 (balance 1 to 0), and remains correct; its support rises from 8 to 10 for actor 5. There is no future planning model.

11. **Policy comparison and costs:** the tables preserve full matched worlds and both baselines. Better correct-ballot coverage can accompany lower conditional accuracy. At wrong-verdict cost 1 and zero effort price, Always's mean paired utility gain against Never is 0.375 per series; ConflictDriven 0.3125, CostSensitive 0.21875 and CredibilityAware 0.28125. At effort price 1/4 these become -1.320312, -0.539062, -0.328125 and -0.421875. At all preregistered wrong costs 1/2/4 and nonzero effort prices 1/4 or 1, each active policy's aggregate utility gain against Never is negative in this sample. These are declared valuation assumptions, not objective measures of cognition or welfare.

15. **Hypotheses and unsupported claims:** H1-H7 are supported as narrow programmed-mechanism demonstrations by executed controls/tests, with adverse H3 retained. ## Representative causal failures and limits

Accurate seed 0/Always: actor 1, observer 1 begins with scores(actor 1=1, wrong 2=2), pays one token and privately changes to(actor 1=3, wrong 2=2). After explicit reports replace earlier source branches and the same artifact is deduplicated, its final vote is abstention. Episode 2 has no tokens left and four observers abstain. Across the four Accurate seeds, Always ends with 44 correct ballots versus Never 52, despite 40 locally improved acquisitions. Explicit environment-report branches are excluded from source credibility grading, so later history differs; the precise full policy effect includes communication and memory consequences. This is not an isolated-clue accuracy estimate.

Misleading seed 0/Always: the physical trace says 2 when actor 1 caused the incident. Observer 0 begins(actor 1=2, wrong 2=1), receives(actor 1=2, wrong 2=3), and changes its belief to 2. The second-round artifact reports produce five wrong ballots for 2 and an incorrect verdict. The engine's actor remains 1; content accuracy and classification are observer fields. Faithful reporting of a false artifact is not automatically intentional lying.

Ordinary inference reaches the inherited 005 evidence-depth limit 866 times across primary runs, including 120 held-out proposals. Each rejection consumes the communication opportunity and emits no testimony; ballots remain resolved legally. Investigation rejections are zero. Exact acquired reports have direct acquisition ancestry instead of unrelated inference parents; exact received reports keep a depth-three parent cap. This bounded communication failure remains measured rather than silently permitting arbitrary depth.

The fields called inspections are deterministic input-item exposure proxies: received messages/findings/report payloads counted once per neutral assessment and twice for weighted-plus-neutral assessment, across action, investigation and delivery assessments. Their primary sum is 531006; they do not count every nested traversal or machine instruction. Maximum retained records 8, findings 1 and received messages 12 are observed primary maxima, not every configuration's limits. Full-report serialization totals 566158382 bytes (maximum 1207759), final memories 16790840 bytes total (maximum 34236 per series). Compact primary evidence is 61244605 bytes; these are serialized-storage proxies, not measured heap or wall-clock benchmarks.

Weights, source association, branch remapping, exclusion from credit ingestion and source-first inspection are research assumptions. Four-vote majority, silence, legal abstention and conflicting support can yield no verdict. Source quality and behavior drift are predetermined experimental world controls, not initiatives by investigated sources. Bounds are intentionally small; memory eviction and imperfect authenticated verification retain historical 007 semantics. Artifact fabrication, sophisticated lie selection, broader learning, alternative responsibility/weights, real verifier security and 009 remain open.
