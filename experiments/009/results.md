# Experiment 009 findings

Implemented, evaluated and frozen on Windows, 2026-10-10. Preregistration `621e668` preceded implementation and held-out execution. No selection formula, threshold, belief rule or held-out generator distribution was tuned after examining outcomes. These are consequences of programmed policies under controlled worlds, not emergent purpose or calibrated information value.

## Population and causal evidence

[Frozen evidence](../../evidence/experiment-009.json) contains 960 primary series/2880 incidents, each executed twice with identical complete reports and six canonical section hashes. There are 448 diagnostic series (16 families *4 seeds *7 policies) and 512 generated series (64 held-out worlds *7 policies plus one free-injection control). Seeds 3000-3063 are disjoint from development 0-15. Independent inference units are 64 entire worlds, not ballots or repeated treatments. Diagnostic summaries do not enter held-out bootstrap intervals.

Separate evidence holds four selected/forced/injected triples (12 one-incident series) and 64 observer-only diagnostic comparisons: 184 alternative series plus 128 ordinary selector series, each with three incidents and full replay. In total 1284 series/3828 incidents per evaluation pass have full canonical section coverage. Counterfactuals change only observer 5's episode 0 acquisition, keeping other investigators at Never; later selector decisions are explicitly forced to decline to match alternatives. Forced choices are controller interventions, never initiative. Twelve selected/forced/injected runs deliver the same finding and local assessment; selected/forced spend one unit while injection spends zero, all yielding 5 correct/0 wrong/1 abstaining ballot per incident.

Each primary row stores an environment digest over identical latent content/access/prices/observations/budgets/communication settings, excluding the declared investigation treatment. All held-out pairs match it. Downstream messages, local memory and ballots can differ and are measured. Repeated diagnostic worlds under differently named families (for example MisleadingExpensive and IncorrectPublicCharacteristics, and QualityVsCost and SelectedVsInjected) are descriptive controls, never additional independent generalization units.

## Resource valuation and forecasts

Under wrong-verdict penalty 1 and zero resource price, per-world utility difference versus FirstAccessible is-0.171875 for InformationValue and-0.109375 for BudgetAware. At price 0.25 it becomes+2.546875 and+2.941406; at price 1,+10.703125 and+12.093750. Positive cost-sensitive comparisons chiefly reflect saved effort, not better factual judgment. Against Never at price 0.25, both lose declared utility (-1.472656/-1.078125); every active policy loses aggregate utility to Never at both nonzero prices for all wrong penalties 1/2/4. Full sensitivity at nine declared valuations is frozen. Utility means have no separate utility confidence interval; accuracy/coverage/risk intervals are world-clustered, and no statistically robust utility-superiority claim is made.

Held-out InformationValue spends 385/160=2.40625 units per candidate-changing selected finding; FirstAccessible 1081/158=6.84177 and BudgetAware 300/135=2.22222. This includes unsuccessful spending and does not equate a change with factual improvement. Cost per locally correct-candidate improvement is 385/45=8.55556 versus 1081/48=22.52083 and 300/40=7.5. These are repeated fixed-local-input effects in paired worlds, not independent samples or incremental whole-policy accuracy. Against Never, BudgetAware's nine additional correct ballots cost 300/9=33.33333 units; InformationValue has five fewer correct ballots, so its incremental-correct-ballot denominator is nonpositive and efficiency is undefined. Extra units per incremental correct ballot/verdict are explicitly null when gains are nonpositive or a policy saves units.

InformationValue's238 held-out acquisitions include93 correct, 107 incorrect and 38 missing findings; 45 locally improve correct-candidate status, 39 worsen it and 154 leave that status unchanged. Its mean declared V is 535.04/1000; 160/238 change candidate, while78/238 have a positive estimate with no realized candidate change. BudgetAware's199 acquisitions include87 correct, 81 incorrect, 31 missing; 40 improve, 22 worsen, 137 are factually unchanged. 135 change candidate and 64 have a positive estimate without change. All selected heuristic estimates were positive by their programmed gate. A nonzero expectation followed by no change is not inherently irrational; forecasts are fallible assumptions, not guaranteed outcomes. Candidate change can be harmful, and support scores are not probabilities.

## Answers to the research questions

1. **Meaningful alternatives:** yes. Primary opportunities 17280 contain 29640 accessible and 21794 affordable option exposures; 7850 opportunities offer at least two legal targets. Individuals select 7423 inspections and voluntarily decline 9857, with zero rejected investigations. Held-out selections use all three prices: FirstAccessible 346/174/129 acquisitions at price 1/2/3, InformationValue 130/69/39 and BudgetAware 121/55/23. Categories and actual source IDs are frozen beside each decision.

2. **Visible characteristics:** yes as programmed-mechanism evidence. An executed matched development control holds local evidence/belief/budget fixed, changes advertised quality 90 to 5 for source 1, and switches all six InformationValue choices from source 1 to source 0. Local tests separately vary described candidate coverage and disclosed observation overlap. Catalogs contain no uninspected result, actor, current accuracy or hidden root. Instrument specifications are claims, not oracle accuracy.

3. **Actual prices:** yes. Tests enforce charges 1/2/3 independently in the resolver, including false/missing results; an unaffordable price 2 proposal with balance 1 is rejected without inspecting its affordable price 1 alternative. CostReversal swaps only prices: FirstAccessible yields 44 correct/4 wrong/24 abstain over 72 ballots versus QualityVsCost 60/0/12, while Cheapest yields 60/0/12 and Random 68/0/4. Price changes can alter both selection and future affordability.

4. **Conservation:** yes. Primary 18144 initial units=10151 spent+7993 retained, no replenishment/refund/transfer. All per-person ledgers conserve. In held-out comparisons each policy starts 1281 units; InformationValue retains 896 and BudgetAware 981 versus FirstAccessible 200. Scarcity can legally prevent all further acquisition.

5. **Better than first-accessible:** context-dependent, not established generally. QualityVsCost diagnostics give InformationValue 72 correct/0 wrong/0 abstain over 72 ballots at 64 units, versus First 60/0/12 at 72. Held-out InformationValue is worse on aggregate correct ballots, verdicts and conditional accuracy. The paired table preserves the 16 improved/20 worsened/28 unchanged worlds against First.

6. **Better than random:** not generally. InformationValue gains 8 correct ballots over Random in held-out worlds but has lower conditional accuracy and an interval spanning zero; 18 worlds improve, 19 worsen and 27 tie. BudgetAware gains 22 correct ballots with 24/17/23 worlds, also an interval spanning zero. EqualSources Random reaches 72/0/0 at 72 units while InformationValue reaches 60/0/12 at the same cost. Random can distribute acquisitions across distinct artifacts, altering downstream corroboration, even when both latent results agree.

7. **Failures:** inaccurate quality claims, missing results, misleading expensive evidence, strong initial certainty, early depletion, hidden dependence and inherited communication/credit rules all matter. MisleadingExpensive InformationValue yields 52 correct/20 wrong/0 abstain and four wrong verdicts at 64 units, versus Cheapest 60/0/12 and zero wrong verdicts at 72. Held-out Cheapest also exceeds InformationValue by 27 correct ballots and six correct verdicts, with six fewer wrong verdicts, while spending 620 additional units. Saving resources and being more accurate are distinct objectives.

8. **Complexity justified:** no broad accuracy or runtime benefit established. Cost-sensitive declared utilities can favor selective heuristics, but simpler policies can be factually better. All policies enumerate possible outcomes for common audits: held-out hypothetical-assessment counts 5214 for First versus 5204 for both value policies. These are not incremental cognitive-performance benchmarks. First's full-report bytes 63968610 versus 60299435/59969935 and final-memory bytes 1781054 versus 1869548/1871295 partly reflect different histories; serialized storage is not measured heap or CPU efficiency.

9. **Expected versus realized:** counts above distinguish positive V, candidate change and factual effects. Across all 7423 primary selected findings, 1006 positive estimates have no candidate change and 12 zero estimates have a change (the latter are baseline selections, not strict-value gates). 4497 selected acquisitions change candidate; 620 have no candidate or support effect. More evidence may change support without changing the final vote. Among correct selected held-out findings, 720 final executed ballots equal their acquisition-time candidate; this is a trace equality, not proof that a matched no-acquisition ballot would be identical.

10. **Known dependence:** can reduce expected value but does not ensure complete avoidance or improved accuracy. A local source claim plus a visibly derived record contributes max(1, 2)=2, not 3; an already settled known candidate has estimated V 0. KnownDuplicate gives InformationValue 20 inspections/40 units/68 correct/4 abstaining ballots versus HiddenDuplicate44/64/72/0 across four seeds. Visible dependence avoids24 inspections and 24 units here but four additional abstentions remain. A known duplicate can still be selected because 008's weight replacement can resolve a tie; no novel independent source is manufactured. Incorrect confidence/association assumptions in this inherited grouping rule remain a limitation; 009 does not introduce deliberately false provenance declarations.

11. **Hidden dependence:** remains inaccessible. Primary 426 delivered findings have undisclosed observation ancestry versus 305 disclosed dependent findings. A hidden record and its source testimony can appear as two groups, yielding 1+2=3, while visible dependence yields 2. Equal-view tests change actual root/content/actor without changing pre-acquisition choices for all seven policies. SeparateAcquisition means explicit acquisition metadata, not proven statistical independence of sources sharing an incident.

12. **Future options:** yes. Primary 1672 opportunities, including 215 held-out First versus 63 InformationValue/36 BudgetAware, have no affordable source because earlier spending lowered a balance that initially could afford one. This is an affordability proxy, not automatically a missed useful opportunity. ResourceScarcity seed 0 observer 5: InformationValue spends both units in episode 0 on V 450 evidence that confirms its already correct candidate, then cannot inspect the episode 1 conflict. BudgetAware's reservation makes that first score 0 and declines; it later pays 2 on V 900 evidence, resolving the tie correctly. Across four scarcity seeds BudgetAware has 72 correct ballots at 36 units; InformationValue 52 correct/4 wrong/16 abstain at 48 and First 48/4/20 at 48. This supports bounded reservation, not foresight or an optimal planner.

13. **Communication and voting:** full-policy effects are not fixed-dialogue comparisons. Against Never, InformationValue changes communication in21/64 worlds and ballots in33; BudgetAware 15/28. Against First the counts are 32/42 and 32/45. Primary 1669 inherited depth-limit communication proposals are rejected, including 128 held-out InformationValue and 132 BudgetAware; their scheduled turns terminate normally. Explicit reports preserve source identity, actual selected intent stays observer-only, and ordinary influence/credit can reinforce or suppress later decisions. 1951 primary informative acquisitions remain private without accepted subsequent speech from their owner.

14. **Windows/Linux replay:** actual Windows regeneration and frozen regression match the entire evaluation and 1284 complete section sets. An independent Python sorted-integer canonical reference also matches evaluation/metric hashes. Linux did not execute: WSL is not installed, Docker absent, CI untriggered. Cross-platform agreement remains unverified. See validation.md and the separate 009 workflow.

15. **Historical integrity:** yes. Complete pre-freeze 155-test gate passes all 001-008 frozen contracts; the additional frozen 009 regression passes, yielding 156 tests individually validated (six unit/150 integration). Historical source/tests/toolchain/lock and 11 artifact hashes are unchanged. No single post-freeze 156-test full-suite run is claimed: the complete 155-test gate and added regression are separate executions.

16. **Git record:** dedicated branch codex/experiment-009.621e668 preregistration; 6feb695 implementation/design clarifications; 6f4ee9a fixture metadata correction together with invariant tests and CI; 5102065 final metadata/source-generation assertions; ec98fa2 frozen evidence/Windows manifest/new regression. The final documentation commit is listed in the completion response and Git log. Actual Windows execution records source revision 5102065b2e0c8cb855f8af0d53c6d33f620b97f3. No historical rewrite or push; 010 not started.

17. **Unsupported claims:** calibrated beliefs or V forecasts, universal targeting superiority, learned source quality, psychological realism, emergent intelligence, optimal resource planning, causal-root discovery, external generality, hostile-host sandboxing, runtime/RAM efficiency and Linux agreement. H1-H7 have narrow executed mechanism support; H8 has positive diagnostics and resource-price-sensitive utility contrasts, alongside held-out factual failures. It is not established as a general decision improvement.
