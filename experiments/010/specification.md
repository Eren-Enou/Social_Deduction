## Questions and hypotheses

H1 legally inspected results plus accepted outcome verification change local method accuracy estimates; inspection alone changes only observed missingness. H2 different received histories change estimates and later source choices with identical present evidence/catalog/budget. H3 historical estimates may predict future source accuracy under stationarity, but advertisements, selective exposure, scarce samples, feedback faults and regime reversal can defeat them. H4 prediction, source selection, local belief, final votes and resource-adjusted utility are separate outcomes; none is assumed to improve generally. H5 hidden content/parameters/roots cannot change pre-acquisition choices with equal local histories. H6 disclosed dependence and duplicate feedback prevent duplicated eligible samples without discovering hidden ancestry. H7 bounded retained conflict quarantine can undo retained accuracy contributions, but never reconstruct evicted detail or rewrite decisions. H8 exploration changes exposures and pays real costs, including wasted or harmful acquisitions. Falsification includes unchanged controlled local estimates/choices, illegal updates, information leaks, nondeterministic replay, or unsupported comparative improvement. Null and adverse H3/H4 results do not invalidate an implemented local learning mechanism.

## Method identity, world and locality

Each incident-local source has a distinct namespaced artifact identity plus a publicly disclosed recurring family key (versioned ASCII identifier, 1–32 characters). Maximum four families per series and four artifacts per incident. Family names/order/category do not encode quality. Accessible catalogs legally disclose family, specification claims, cost, coverage and visible provenance, never the current result, actual accuracy/missingness parameters or hidden root. The observer registry separately records generation parameters and regime schedule. An unfamiliar family starts without experienced credit. All latent artifacts and observations are initialized before policy treatment; content cannot depend on inspection or selection. A new physical artifact receives a fresh incident-specific random draw; derived fixtures copy a declared observation, not a conveniently regenerated clue.

Investigation input contains the unchanged 009 local view plus only that observer's bounded method summaries and encountered family mappings. Ordinary dialogue contains neither method-estimator state nor verification schedules. Received environmental reports can be recorded as indirect experience only when their family is legitimately recognizable from the recipient's own accessible source description; otherwise their family is unknown. Indirect reports never add personal accuracy or missingness samples in this version. Speaker credibility and method reliability have separate storage, updater inputs and effects. Method estimates influence target evaluation only, never testimony weights or the strength of an acquired finding.

## Bounded experience and feedback

Each observer retains eight FIFO direct acquisition records, four FIFO indirect encounter records, at most four family summaries, eight retained episode receipts and 32 token journal entries. Direct records contain family, acquisition/episode/source references, delivered candidate or missing result, visible dependency key, selected/forced/injected origin, dedup eligibility and verification state. Summaries are compact consequences with bounded u16 success/failure/nonmissing/missing counts, not archived payloads. Explicit eviction events fold accepted contributions into a checkpoint before removing detail; accuracy is recomputed from checkpoint plus retained accepted records. Count operations saturate u16, never wrap. No automatic decay or recency weighting is introduced. A regime change can therefore leave estimates stale.

Deduplicate direct samples using recipient-visible episode/artifact identity or disclosed observation-parent identity, across selected and injected deliveries. First eligible encounter owns the sample; a second visible shared-origin acquisition does not grant an additional family a new independent sample. Hidden shared ancestry remains apparently distinct. Pending/unverifiable records remain ungraded. Missing outcomes contribute to observed missingness, never to inaccurate-candidate counts. Each nonmissing direct record contributes at most once to accuracy after eligible accepted verification.

Use actual `token-007-v1` transport and unchanged ConflictAware acceptance through an eligibility-only projection of retained method records: synthetic local references declare which episodes remain available, with no speaker responsibility or candidate assertions. This projection is derived exclusively from own retained acquisition records; it is not an objective history lookup. Reuse recognized issuer/channel, session/serial binding, future/absent episode checks, repeated agreement and sticky retained contradiction quarantine. Map its accepted receipt back to own retained candidates for method grading. Authenticated false outcomes can corrupt counts; no fault label enters a recipient. Quarantine removes affected retained accuracy samples but preserves directly observed missingness. When all records from an episode are evicted, later feedback cannot repair, grade or undo their checkpoint contributions. Feedback received without eligible records is logged and never attached retroactively. Historical snapshots remain immutable.

Feedback plans declare after-episode, about-episode, token and recipients; maximum 32 plans and four issuance positions per episode. Generated clean verification arrives after completed incidents. Diagnostics include missing, selective, delayed, false authenticated, repeated, conflicting, unauthenticated, malformed association and late-eviction feedback. The same delivered token may update the existing interpersonal subsystem separately, under its unchanged eligibility rules; instrument records do not become speaker experiences.

## Exact estimator and target policies

Accuracy targets factual correctness conditional on a nonmissing candidate: Beta(1,1) smoothed frequency `(success+1)/(success+failure+2)`. Missingness targets an inspected method returning no candidate: `(missing+1)/(missing+nonmissing+2)`. Both store exact numerator/denominator and eligible counts; zero observations give 1/2, not learned accuracy. Replaceable updater receives only one observer's local memory, acquired result and actually delivered verification. Prediction is a programmed model, not calibrated belief or human learning.

Ten primary selectors: NeverInvestigate, FirstAccessible, CheapestAvailable, SeededRandomTarget, Frozen009InformationValue, Frozen009BudgetAware, AdvertisedQuality, ExperienceAdjusted, ConservativeLearner and ExploratoryLearner. Frozen selectors call unchanged 009 policies, formulas and stream initialization on the current 009-shaped view; episode/balance/remaining opportunities describe the new longer series. AdvertisedQuality uses unchanged 009 information-value selection and is an explicit duplicate negative control, not an independent mechanism or sample.

ExperienceAdjusted substitutes its rational learned accuracy/missingness into the public-description quality fields used by unchanged 009 possible-result enumeration, rounding down to integer percentages, then uses its strict `V-100*actual_price > 0` gate. Capture original claims, exact learned probabilities, effective integer inputs, hypothetical outcomes and choice. No actual current result is consulted. ConservativeLearner uses advertisement until three eligible accuracy samples, then Beta(2,2) accuracy; missingness uses advertisement until three direct samples, then Beta(2,2). Experience dependence for accuracy and missingness is independently logged. This threshold/prior is fixed, not tuned. ExploratoryLearner normally uses ExperienceAdjusted; on every fourth own investigation opportunity it selects the affordable accessible family with fewest direct eligible samples, ties lower actual price then semantic method key, even if the exploitation value gate would decline. Exploration remains optional when no legal option exists and never uses hidden quality. Record the unmodified exploitation proposal, exploration flag and opportunity counter. Other ties and provenance assumptions remain 009 rules.

Before each selection, forecast all accessible methods, including unaffordable/unselected alternatives, from neutral 1/2, advertisement, experienced Beta(1,1), effective selected-policy expectations and observer process parameters. The observer benchmark and actual labels are attached only after captured local forecasts. Score nonmissing correctness conditionally; score missingness separately for all defined alternatives. Do not score a missing candidate as incorrect. Selected-only, all-accessible, stationary/changed, before/after shift, sample-count and verification-fault populations have separate denominators. Actual hidden process parameters never enter policy input.
