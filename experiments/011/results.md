# Experiment 011 findings

Individuals voluntarily transmitted reports of method experience, retained received testimony separately from personal evidence, and changed later paid investigations through bounded local interpretations. Visible provenance reduced known duplication in controlled probes. Hidden copying, sincere error, stale advice and credibility weighting produced adverse outcomes. **This evaluation does not establish a general social-learning advantage over direct learning.**

## Executed protocol

[Specification](specification.md) was committed before implementation and primary execution. [Evidence](../../evidence/experiment-011.json) contains **952 series / 7,616 incidents**: 768 voluntary held-out series across 64 matched independent worlds, 176 diagnostic series (22 labels, two seeds, four listeners), four selected/injected controls and four executed fixed-current history controls. All 952 series ran twice with identical complete reports; a separate Windows execution independently regenerated the full evaluation and seven hashes per series. See [validation](validation.md) for actual gates and platform limits.

Each held-out cell has 64 worlds, 512 incidents, 3,072 investigation/ballot opportunities, 6,144 speech opportunities, 4,521 accessible method forecasts and 3,898 initial effort units. There are 32 stationary and 32 shifted worlds. Diagnostic labels are controlled questions, not independent population samples; some intentionally share a generator. The held-out treatments have no forced acquisitions or injected reports. Paid training and routing/round constraints in diagnostics and controls are declared controller actions; their later selected reports remain distinct from controller-injected messages.

There are 16 preregistered contrasts, two outcome windows and three regimes: 96 comparisons. Intervals resample entire matched worlds 512 times with seed 11011, sorted endpoints 12/499. Counts, rates and losses are averaged within world before resampling. Undefined denominators remain null and every interval reports its defined-world count. Pooled prediction scores are descriptive and can differ from world means. No multiplicity correction, independent-voter inference or population-general conclusion is claimed. Stationary and shifted groups are different stratified worlds, not identical-world reversal counterfactuals. The additional illustrative hidden-copy and susceptibility extractions below are post hoc descriptions of immutable captured rows, not policy tuning or new evaluation runs.

The source separates own 010 graded/observed counts from social pseudo-support and own 006 interpersonal credit. Reports never transfer an authenticated verification token. Source quality and actual transmission roots enter only postexecution research scoring. A favorable report is an assessment of a method, not an incident accusation. Intent classification is instrumentation of a supplied policy; a false assessment is not automatically an intentional lie.

A synthetic arithmetic-bound test with 50 failures checks that capped social support cannot overpower strong contrary personal evidence; it is not a 50-acquisition lived experiment.

## Answers to the 22 required research questions

1. **Voluntary personal reports?** Yes: 1,590 accepted sincere reports in SincereProvenance, alongside 2,685 locally eligible withholding decisions. Silent and selective alternatives remain legal.
2. **Use without observer knowledge?** Yes through narrow captured views; counts stay personal, claims stay testimony, actual roots/labels are attached only by the observer. Information-locality and hidden-intent tests pass.
3. **Different social histories?** Yes: both executed history pairs change the same-current observer's assessment and A/B selection, with equal own evidence and budget.
4. **Later source selection?** Yes: 142/3,072 held-out sincere-provenance investigations differ from their captured personal proposals; 15 trigger and 70 avoid investigation. AfterInvestigation does not back-edit resolved choices.
5. **Prediction beyond personal learning?** Controlled accurate advice improves some forecasts; full held-out sincere accuracy loss slightly worsens relative to its own personal forecast and comparative intervals include zero. Missingness loss can improve. No general accuracy/calibration advantage demonstrated.
6. **Decision accuracy or utility?** No general advantage: the matched sincere-use contrast has +0.234375 correct ballots/world with interval [-0.171875,0.671875] and utility +0.265625 [-0.101562,0.683593]. Complete sharing versus silence is adverse overall.
7. **How often misleading?** Sincere advice worsens 233/3,403 nonmissing forecast exposures (233/550 changed ones); misleading-control advice worsens 828/3,403 (828/1,566 changed ones). These are prediction losses, not automatic lie classifications. Misleading control yields 715/3,072 wrong ballots.
8. **Repeated false corroboration?** Same-speaker repetition adds no independent support. Separate speakers copying a shared root can: 48 pair exposures in the naive visible-relay diagnostic, 856 concealed pair exposures in held-out misleading control.
9. **Provenance reduction?** Visible-relay diagnostic pair exposures fall 48 to zero; the matched probability falls 4/5 to 2/3. Its judgments remain equal, and ordinary sincere/misleading held-out listener comparisons are null.
10. **Hidden-origin misinformation cascades?** Hidden copying and harmful downstream sequences occur in the held-out misleading control, including the seed5001 trace. The standalone hidden-origin diagnostic copies accurate advice; it proves dependence cannot be detected locally, not misinformation by itself. No isolated general cascade amplification effect is established.
11. **Sincere mistakes?** Yes in SincereInaccurate/CorruptedHistory after wrong accepted feedback; the false assessment is locally sincere, and 60 forecast exposures worsen in each provenance diagnostic.
12. **Deliberately misleading reports?** Held-out misleading versus sincere provenance loses 234 correct ballots and 58 correct verdicts, spends 809 more units and has 334 socially changed investigations. Lower wrong-verdict counts accompany substantially more unresolved outcomes.
13. **Existing credibility?** It harms both controlled cases: amplifies a credible false source and suppresses an unreliable speaker's correct advice. Held-out credible versus provenance has equal verdict counts and uncertain small ballot changes; no broad benefit.
14. **Corrections after spread?** Yes locally where received: observer5's episode6 2/6 versus stale 3/7 expectation and better full-delivery ballots. No retroactive edits, automatic population receipt or guaranteed recovery.
15. **Sparse experience susceptibility?** Larger fixed-message arithmetic influence is built into the bounded formula and tested. Lived exposure strata do not independently establish a monotonic population law; receipt/history differences and small high-grade cells remain confounders.
16. **Changing reliability?** Old positive summaries persist after the declared episode3 reversal and can misdirect investigations until direct feedback/corrections accumulate. All-delivery correction still leaves eight wrong verdicts out of 16 diagnostic incidents. Held-out stationary/shift and assessment results above preserve heterogeneity.
17. **Withheld/unreceived?** In sincere provenance, 2,685/4,275 eligible opportunities withhold; 4,863 of 9,540 potential addressed receipts occur and 4,677 remain unreceived. Limited scope is not silent population knowledge.
18. **Resource costs?** Sincere-only sharing consumes ordinary testimony opportunities and increases spending versus silence; selective sharing reduces reports/evictions. Actual 1-3-unit investigations conserve budgets. Utility changes with declared error/effort prices; no invented speech-token fee.
19. **Historical preservation?** All fifteen raw evidence hashes and historical engine/test/dependency/toolchain bytes remain unchanged; complete-gate details are in validation.
20. **Actual Linux replay?** No. Windows replay and independent canonical reference pass. A Linux x86_64 host or explicit authorized CI push/dispatch is required; workflow existence is not execution.
21. **Git commits?** Preregistration `b689d92`, implementation `ba439e8`, validated data freeze `1333d30`, and the final `docs(experiment-011): report social transmission findings and limitations` documentation commit. Validation records full confirmed prerequisite hashes; the final delivery/Git log records the documentation hash. No push or history rewrite.
22. **Unsupported claims?** No consciousness, human-equivalent intent, emergent social intelligence, calibrated forecasts, optimal strategy, globally verified provenance, general collective benefit, broad sparse-history law, causal population effect of hidden duplication, portability beyond executed Windows, or larger social institutions.

## Scope and remaining tests

H1-H6 establish the specified local transmission/withholding/receipt/provenance/revision/error mechanisms in controlled fixtures and captured traces. H7 remains mixed/adverse and does not establish a general social-learning gain. H8 is supported narrowly by the declared credibility fixtures, not a general reputation model. All policies and grouping/weight rules are supplied deterministic mechanisms; observing their downstream consequences is not evidence of emergent cognition.

The boundaries are trusted typed Rust components, not a security sandbox. The measured built-in investigation composition uses 010 ExperienceAdjusted for the personal proposal and 009 InformationValue for the social-adjusted choice. Social-enabled composition does not preserve arbitrary base Never/Frozen/exploration behavior; DirectOnly retains the configured base choice, and other strategies require custom narrow components or further evaluation. Origins and verification assertions are unverified claims; report transmission roots are not physical statistical-independence certificates. Correction recovery is limited by delivery, retained branches and cumulative counts. No held-out tuning, language models, advanced learning, GUI, global reputation, omniscient truth channel or Experiment 012 was introduced. This experiment's completion criterion is a reliable measured environment, not a positive intelligence demonstration.
