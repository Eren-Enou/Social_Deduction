## Hypotheses and falsification

H1 retained personal experience permits voluntary sincere reporting, while withholding and undelivered reports leave recipients uninformed. H2 identical received histories imply identical interpretations despite hidden intent/origin/truth changes. H3 socially reported experience can change later source choices without adding personal verification samples. H4 visible shared-origin grouping reduces fictitious corroboration compared with a susceptibility baseline; hidden common origins remain inaccessible. H5 own-message corrections/retractions change current local standing only where delivered and retained. H6 sincere error and deliberate contradiction can propagate harmful expectations. H7 social prediction, ballots and declared utility may improve, worsen or remain unchanged relative to direct learning. H8 credibility can amplify misinformation or suppress useful advice. Falsification includes inaccessible evidence use, compulsory reports, invented personal samples, duplicate support from one speaker, mutation of past decisions, nondeterminism, absent controlled influence or unsupported comparative benefit. Null/adverse H7 results are scientifically valid.

An optional acquisition reference can be disclosed as an unverified claim. Payload claims do not prove source truth or independence. ## Fixed sharing and interpretation policies

SilentMethod offers no method report and permits ordinary incident speech. SincereExperienceReport uses a personally retained summary with observations, selecting lexicographically first relevant family; changes to its own prior assessment/counts may select correction, uncertainty may retract. It reports local accepted counts, not actual truth. SelectiveExperienceReport uses the same candidates only with at least two direct observations and benefit 2 greater than local revision cost; ties withhold. UncertainReport reports uncertainty for a recognizable accessible family with fewer than two graded observations or conflicting accepted counts. RelayExperience copies a retained report not already forwarded, preserving public parent/origin; may forward a received correction by revising its own earlier relay. MisleadingReportControl reports the opposite of a definite local assessment or a declared unsupported favorable claim under insufficient evidence. It may conceal a legitimately received copy's origin in the hidden-origin diagnostic. Selected intent remains private to the observer.

DirectOnly ignores social advice and runs unchanged 010 ExperienceAdjusted. NaiveSocial uses separate bounded social pseudo-support: one current branch per immediate speaker/family, so repetition by one speaker never creates independent support. ProvenanceAwareSocial additionally groups recipient-visible parent/origin relationships; disclosed relay copies count once. Missing parent IDs form unverified opaque groups, named/firsthand claims remain unverified associations. Hidden ancestry never groups records. CredibilityWeightedSocial adds only local 006 weight max(0,1+score), 0-3, to these same groups; no shared reputation. Conflicting active reports within a group give zero, conflicting independent groups can cancel.

Each eligible favorable/unfavorable group supplies one positive/negative pseudo-unit (credibility variant 0-3); cap total social influence at four units using proportional integer allocation with ties favoring uncertainty. Effective conditional accuracy = (personal successes+1+positive)/(personal graded+2+positive+negative). Uncertain/retracted/contested claims add zero. Reported 100 successes has no greater weight than reported one success. Availability advice uses reported missing-versus-nonmissing majority, independently capped four units, with personal `(missing+1)/(observed+2)` prior. Social pseudo-units never enter personal counters. Record personal, social-adjusted and effective percentages, local grouping/status, original direct-only proposal and final action. Use floored percentages through unchanged 009 information-value gate. Advice does not change actual finding content, weight or verification.
