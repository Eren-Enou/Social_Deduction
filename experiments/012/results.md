# Experiment 012 findings

Individuals initiated typed, private method requests, voluntarily replied or withheld, and changed some later paid investigations through their legitimately received answers. Communication capacity was conserved. **Targeted sincere inquiry did not outperform ordinary communication, unsolicited sincere sharing or random inquiry under the preregistered default utility.** It consumed more effort, displaced more incident testimony, produced fewer correct ballots/verdicts and increased unresolved outcomes. Some forecast improvements and locally useful answers did not compensate for these costs.

## Executed protocol

The [specification](specification.md) was committed in `596e8a6`; all engineering clarifications and implementation were committed as `9cdbf593dc7361108939e0e6954a024b6b6a0e9d` before any primary world ran. The primary population contains **696 series / 5,568 incidents**: 512 held-out series (eight treatments, 64 independent matched worlds), 176 diagnostic series (22 labels, two seeds, four listeners), four selected/injected controls and four no-request/no-reply controls. Every series ran twice with identical complete reports. A separate actual Windows process independently regenerated that entire population and its manifest. The independent canonical audit matched the evaluation, metric digest and eight section hashes per series, and checked all rational prediction groups, routing, capacity and effort invariants.

The complete current-source gate passed all 266 tests. [Evidence](../../evidence/experiment-012.json) and [Windows replay](../../evidence/experiment-012-windows.json) were frozen through exclusive creation after the successful gate and audits. The separately added frozen regression also passed, for 267 distinct validated tests; formatting and warnings-denied Clippy passed with it included. Exact gate/artifact status is recorded in [validation](validation.md). No Linux execution, push or Experiment 013 occurred.

Each held-out treatment has 64 worlds, 512 incidents, 3,072 ballot/investigation/first-query opportunities, 3,072 final-phase slots and at most 6,144 outbound actions. Initial effort totals **3,828** per treatment; spent plus retained conserves that total. The same latent environments, actual sealed outputs, initial observations/resources, order and routes are shared across treatments. There are 32 stationary and 32 shifted worlds; assessment is episodes 4-7. Held-out acquisitions and answers have no controller injections or forced training. Paid training, constrained routes/type choices, late answers and wider delivery in diagnostics are declared interventions, not participant initiative.

Eleven contrasts across two windows and three regimes produce 66 comparisons. Intervals resample entire matched worlds 512 times with seed 12012, sorted endpoints 12/499, retaining defined-world denominators. Rates and losses are computed within world before resampling. Pooled forecast scores are descriptive and may differ from world means. No independent-voter inference, multiplicity correction, calibration claim or population-general conclusion is justified. The additional fixed-input forecast-change summaries, categorical process-direction checks and illustrative trace extractions below are analyses of immutable captured rows, not policy tuning.

Requester scores use only own counts, accessible affordable methods, received prior reports and declared optional local credit/responsiveness. Unknown private expertise never enters policy inputs. `Targeted` uses the uncertainty heuristic; it is not an optimal targeting planner. Other request policies are covered by mechanism tests/diagnostics. The `Ignored` treatment preserves the complete historical 011 DirectOnly composition, including advertised-quality fallback differences without personal grades. Its contrast with Targeted is therefore not purely an answer-content intervention. Exact same-kind before-answer probes and listener fixtures provide narrower controls.

C/W/A denotes correct/wrong/abstaining ballots; C/W/U denotes correct/wrong/unresolved verdicts. All full rows have the same 3,072 ballots and 512 verdict opportunities. Effort is paid only for investigations. No fictional effort price was added to speech capacity.

Query delivery is not expertise or useful knowledge. Targeted reached recipients with some retained observations on 1,021/2,291 requests (44.56%), with accepted accuracy grades on 786/2,291 (34.31%), and no observations on 1,270/2,291. Random's corresponding observed/graded rates were 409/1,139 (35.91%) and 325/1,139 (28.53%). These are observer-only exposure indicators, not truth/utility labels. Targeted had 345/2,291 legitimate prior-report signals; 1,946 subjects/recipients lacked such a signal. It asked 2,283 reliability, seven basis and one availability question, so the held-out rule barely exercised two supported request types.

Response opportunities are phase selections with a retained unanswered inbox, which can include old requests. Categories classify submitted proposals, including rejected ones; absence means a real opportunity with selected silence. Insufficiency/refusal are sent answers but supply no method support. Targeted delivered 945 assessments and 748 insufficiency replies. Of its 637 definite assessments, 407 agreed with sealed process direction and 230 opposed it; 308 were uncertain. Direction compares favorable/unfavorable to conditional accuracy above/below one-half, not actual utility or calibrated truth. All 352 rejected held-out replies came from Misleading, with `unrecognized method family`; their request references were valid. That control includes 705 contrary and 1,186 unsupported proposals, rather than being a pure true/false rating swap. These failures consumed capacity and remain in the record.

Answers can address older queries. Targeted's 1,693 deliveries do not imply 1,693 current questions answered: 1,328/2,291 lacked an answer to that query in the same incident. Unanswered consumed 2,274 actions without any reply; calling all these actions intrinsically irrational would require an untested alternative-value model.

The controlled observations support these narrower conclusions:

- **Accurate exchange, seed 0 episode 2.** Requester 5 has mixed A grades 1/1 and no B samples. Speaker 0 earned two B successes through paid declared training. Constrained routing made 0 the legal possible respondent; the policy did not discover private expertise from a hidden label.
- **A harmful sincere uncertainty reply, held-out seed 6044 episode 2.** Individual 0 asks 2 about instrument-c at (2, 4), with no own experience. - **Capacity competition.** ReplyDisplaces sends an initial wrong incident statement and a sincere answer, exhausts respondent 0's two actions, and prevents its final retraction proposal after a paid B finding. - **Insufficiency and overload.** NoResponse sends 12 queries, no replies and has no answer-induced choice changes. ## Answers to the 24 requested research questions

1. **Voluntary targeted initiation:** yes, 2,291/3,072 opportunities under Targeted; NeverAsk, RandomAsk and silence remain available. This is a programmed choice rule.
2. **Local uncertainty:** scores and captured options depend on own sparse/mixed estimates and close available estimates; controlled input tests establish sensitivity, not spontaneous curiosity.
3. **Recipient choice without privilege:** yes, only legal routes, prior received claims and optional own credit/responsiveness enter the narrow interface. Hidden responder-history substitutions leave the requester choice unchanged.
4. **Useful recipients:** 1,021/2,291 had observed experience and 786/2,291 had grades at request delivery; usefulness/truth is not guaranteed. 945 assessments reached requesters, including 230 definite ratings opposite current process direction.
5. **Responses:** Targeted submitted 637 sincere definite, 308 uncertain and 748 insufficient answers; Selective selected 1,103 silences and 380 explicit refusals; Misleading proposed 705 contrary and 1,186 unsupported assessments with 352 total rejections. Counts/denominators and deliveries are separated above.
6. **Relevance/repetition:** legitimate content was exchanged, but duplicate/grouped advice and repeated inquiries often added no support. Basis/availability are technically supported and diagnostic-tested but barely selected in the primary Targeted policy.
7. **Investigation change:** yes, 53/3,072 fixed-input changes under Targeted; 13 triggers, 27 avoidances, 13 redirects. These are distinct from full-trajectory source differences.
8. **Factual judgment:** locally both improvement and harm occurred. Full Targeted lost 264 correct ballots/66 correct verdicts versus Ordinary and 127/31 versus Random; default-utility intervals are adverse.
9. **Testimony displacement:** yes, 727 exhausted-final builtin speaking proposals and substantially fewer executed ordinary statements. Requests also replace first-phase statements.
10. **Displacement consequences:** the reply fixture prevents an actual retraction opportunity; complete treatments change ballots/coverage/effort, but do not isolate the causal value of each lost statement.
11. **Versus random:** no demonstrated advantage; default utility -0.921875 [-1.519531,-0.226562] per world, coverage and conditional accuracy lower.
12. **Versus unsolicited:** no default decision/utility advantage; -2.195312 [-3.015625,-1.339843] utility, despite the narrower missingness score improvement.
13. **Unanswered capacity:** 2,274 actions with no reply in Unanswered; 1,328/2,291 Targeted queries receive no same-incident answer. Possible later answers/zero-value alternative silence prevent treating every one as provably irrational waste.
14. **Credibility targeting:** controlled local credit can prefer a misinformed source or overlook useful information. No general targeting superiority was measured.
15. **Personal protection:** the same bad answer produces different choices with different legitimate direct histories; that is a controlled mechanism result, not universal immunity or a causal population-wide protection estimate.
16. **Known provenance:** yes, known copying stays one support group in the declared listener fixture; it does not establish authenticity.
17. **Hidden dependencies:** yes, concealed copying yields two visible groups and higher pseudo-confidence despite shared actual ancestry. Hidden roots are not exposed to listeners.
18. **Corrections:** later received revisions alter retained standing without editing old messages. Changed later query options/subjects and delayed revisions occur; no independent beneficial inquiry-recovery estimate was demonstrated.
19. **Forecast value:** no robust general accuracy improvement. One missingness contrast improves while decisions worsen; fixed-input sincere forecast effects include zero and misleading answers worsen accuracy.
20. **Priced benefit:** Targeted is worse on the declared default versus the principal baselines. Some unpriced high-wrong-cost means are positive but intervals cross zero; they do not establish robust benefit.
21. **Historical preservation:** all 17 evidence sizes/hashes and historical engines/tests/workflows/dependencies/document prefixes were retained; actual gates are in validation.
22. **Linux:** no actual Linux execution. WSL is absent and no Docker executable is available; a matching Linux runner or authorized CI/push is required.
23. **Commits:** preregistration `596e8a6` and source `9cdbf593` precede execution. Data commit `44d044e5` preserves evidence and the passed regression. The documentation commit and final state are reported in Git and the delivery message.
24. **Unsupported broader claims:** optimal questioning, calibrated belief, spontaneous curiosity, strategic persuasion, psychological realism, consciousness, global trust, reliable collective truth discovery, robust recovery and general social intelligence remain unsupported.

## Remaining limits

The implemented heuristics are intentionally simple and source/recipient tie-breaking can favor low identifiers. Single-phase replies can answer an old inbox entry rather than the newest question, and private revisions can refer to a branch the new recipient never received, limiting interpretation. Declared family recognition can reject unsupported advice about a family unavailable in the respondent's own catalog/history. These are preserved measured constraints, not silently repaired using primary outcomes.

All four historical listeners remain replaceable and are diagnosed, but the primary grid does not fully cross every request/response/listener kind. Whole-world comparisons include altered downstream claims, personal learning and routes' realized effects; they are not fixed-message causal controls. Limited order/recipient variation does not prove identity symmetry. Eight incidents, six individuals, conditioned fixtures, coarse scalar support and finite memories do not establish large-scale robustness or human behavior. No policy was retuned after observing these outcomes. No 013 mechanism was started.
